# Experimental Data

This folder contains the complete dataset and statistical analysis for the ESP32 soil-moisture sensor calibration study.

## Files

- `soil_moisture_calibration_raw_data.csv`  
  Contains 55 measured ADC readings from 11 independently prepared soil samples across three experimental rounds.

- `soil_moisture_calibration_results.xlsx`  
  Contains the raw-data table, sample-level statistics, condition-level analysis, experimental protocol, limitations, and calibration graph.

## Experimental Conditions

Water additions of 0 g, 10 g, 20 g, 30 g, and 40 g were tested. The main conditions of 0 g, 20 g, and 40 g each included three independently prepared soil samples. Five ADC readings were recorded from each sample.

The results show that the ADC reading decreased consistently as the amount of added water increased.
